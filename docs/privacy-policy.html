Official Policy

Privacy Policy for DartNest

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 | Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Package Name: com.dartnest.app

Summary: DartNest is built as an offline-first educational application. We do NOT collect, sell, or share any personal information. All your learning progress, quiz attempts, and code lab work remain strictly on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to DartNest ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). DartNest is an interactive learning platform designed to teach Flutter and Dart programming through structured lessons, interactive coding sandboxes, quizzes, and practical challenges. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

DartNest is designed with privacy by design:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, register, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use DartNest.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, contact details, physical locations, or device identifiers.
  • Local Data Storage: All user state—such as completed lessons, quiz scores, custom code snippets, theme settings, and practical task progress—is stored locally on your device via standard browser LocalStorage and IndexedDB APIs. This data never leaves your device.

3. Device Permissions

DartNest requires minimal device permissions to operate:

  • INTERNET: Used solely to load local web assets within the native Android WebView container and service workers for offline caching. No background data is transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

DartNest contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral monitoring tools, or external analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook SDK). We do not monetize your data or show advertisements.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is suitable for students, beginners, and developers of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, DartNest complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child safety standards.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all progress data is stored strictly on your local device, you maintain 100% control over your data at all times. You can delete all stored progress, snippets, and exam records at any moment by:

  • Using the "Reset Progress" feature in the Settings tab within DartNest.
  • Clearing the app's cache and storage via Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > DartNest > Storage > Clear Storage).
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.

7. Security

We value your trust in using DartNest. Since all your data resides securely in the application's isolated sandboxed storage provided by the Android OS, unauthorized third-party apps cannot access your learning data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
